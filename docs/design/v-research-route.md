# Configuration V on the research route, planned in F33 (design)

Status: design, 2026-11-24, revised after an independent review. Nothing here is built yet. Register: thesis D61 (V returns to RQ4 on the research route, planned in F33). D61 withdraws the D58 amendment ("V leaves the thesis") and D59's V-free planning. The build is F33, which is not yet built or qualified. These changes join its scope before its gate and guest qualification.

## What the register asks

V (plain Pi, no tbound) runs the 100 compliant tasks, paired with B and C, on the research provider with the same pinned model and D50 settings. RQ4 reports V to B (plumbing), B to C (governance) and V to C (total) as medians of per-task differences. V never runs an adversarial case, never affects H1 and has no rerun. No V unit runs on an H1 compliant task before `T2`. The owner decides V's spend after the research pilot. A no-go switches V off without a re-freeze.

## F33 changes

1. **Plan.** Bring back the six-order V/B/C compliant schedule that `042c7972` (#272) removed from `supervisor/internal/runner/plan.go`, behind one `runner.Config` field that is part of the plan digest. With the field off, the plan is today's V-free plan, step for step. With it on, the plan is F32's compliant shape. B precedes C in exactly 50 of 100 tasks (orders 0, 1 and 4 of the six, with 17, 17 and 16 tasks), and V precedes B in 49.
2. **Counts and report.** `evalreport.PlannedUnits(m)` takes only the manifest today (`report.go:106`). Its callers are `runner/plan.go:212`, `evalreport/report.go:145`, `evalreport/build.go:338`, `tbound-profile/preregistration_linux.go:149` and `tbound-run/corpus.go:98`. Carry the field to it in the report inputs (3 or 2 compliant units per task, 1,935 or 1,835 in the legacy shape, headroom 97 or 92), so that `tbound-run`, `tbound-profile preregistration-record` and the registration record agree. `evalreport/markdown.go:85` prints "V: not planned (D58 amendment)" when the V task count equals the V missing count. Key that line on the field, so a planned V with every unit lost reads as lost. A compliant-screening plan stays B only.
3. **V's provider route.** Today `supervisor/cmd/tbound/trial_vrun_linux.go:305` hands the OpenCode Go key file to the container, and Pi computes the cost from its own price table (`internal/vrun/usage.go`). The research ledger takes an exclusive flock that the research service holds for its whole life (`internal/usdledger/ledger.go:12`). So the forwarder runs inside the research service and uses that service's ledger handle. A forwarder in the `trial v-run` process would get `ErrLocked`. Per V unit:
   - **Key.** No key in the container, its environment or its mounts.
   - **Listener and credential.** The service opens a listener for this unit, which the container can reach, and a random credential that exists only for this unit. Pi gets the credential as its API key and the listener as its provider base URL. Both close when the unit ends.
   - **Request shape.** The forwarder accepts one method and path, the research endpoint's chat-completions POST. It keeps an allowlist of request members (the messages, tools and tool choice Pi sends) and drops every other member and every client header, so `n`, `max_tokens`, `reasoning`, `stop`, `usage`, `models`, `provider`, `route`, `transforms` and `plugins` never reach the provider. It pins `model` and sets the registered `max_completion_tokens` and the frozen profile members.
   - **Input bound.** It refuses a request whose serialized body plus `InputFramingReserveTokens` exceeds `max_input_tokens` (`researchprofile/profile.go:125-133`), or whose body exceeds `openrouter.MaxRequestBytes`, as B and C are bounded. The worst-case reservation (`researchroute/route.go:343`) then stays an upper bound. An actual cost above a reservation is a `SettleOverage`, after which the ledger refuses every Reserve for its whole life (`usdledger/ledger.go:17-19`), so an unbounded V request could end H1 as well. The bound rules out an overage by size. The cost-anomaly path (`route.go:476-493`) is shared with B and C and stays as it is. Reuse the route's own reservation check (`ReserveSpend`, `route.go:363-410`, `ErrInputOverBound`) rather than writing the bound twice. Pi builds the messages and tools; `broker/openrouter` builds its own body (`BuildRequestForRoute`, `openrouter.go:186`), so the forwarder rewrites Pi's body to the profile and shares only the profile constants and the upstream transport.
   - **Budget.** It reserves in `usdledger` before sending and settles after. It refuses every request beyond the case's manifest `max_host_calls`, counting each request that reaches the forwarder, retries included. That is 11 for compliant and ambiguous cases (D50 amendment), the field admission reads (`corpusadmit/trials.go:110-122`). It is not the profile's 17 and not a fixed number. B and C make at most that many requests too: every provider call, retry, resample and tool effect takes one host call (`workflowrecovery/recovery_ledger.go:258-265`, `:321-337`), so their requests stay at or below `max_host_calls`, and 6 is only their clean path of 5 tool turns and a final reply. Stated limit: B's and C's tool effects use up host calls, and V's tools do not, so V can take more tool turns within the same request count. B and C also switch to a tools-disabled final call near the end of the budget (D56), and V, as plain Pi, does not. A V call still open at unit end is settled from its response or stays booked at its worst case, as every governed call does.
   - **Cost, calls and tokens.** The unit's cost is the ledger's. The forwarder records each call and its token usage from the response it parses to settle, and that record is RQ4's source for V's calls and tokens (the ledger stores only micro-USD). At unit end the service returns that record, and `trial v-run` writes the unit's calls, input and output tokens and cost into the V document (`internal/vrun/vrun.go:35`). Pi's own figures (`internal/vrun/usage.go:125`) stay there as a cross-check and are not reported.
   - **Stated limit.** During the unit, the agent can use the forwarder within these limits.
4. **Timing.** RQ4 reads wall time per unit from the runner journal's `elapsed_ms` (set in `internal/runner/runner.go:312`) for V, B and C alike, not from `Metrics.LatencyMS`. Provider time is not split out; this is a documented limit (D61 item 5). No new timing field goes into the governed path.
5. **Behaviours to cover.** Per AGENTS.md, tests are added when the owner asks. The PR that builds these names each behaviour:
   - Plan: both field values, step counts, the 50/50 B-before-C split, and the off value equal to the current plan digest.
   - Counts and report: `PlannedUnits` and the Markdown line for both values.
   - Forwarder: no key in the container; a wrong path, method or model is refused; members outside the allowlist are dropped; a body over `max_input_tokens` (with the framing reserve) or over `MaxRequestBytes` is refused before any reservation; the reservation is booked before the upstream call; the request after `max_host_calls` is refused; the usage record matches the response and reaches the V document; the listener and credential are dead after the unit.
6. **Guest qualification.** One V route probe on a development compliant task, next to G1, rp and dr. The forwarder runs against a fake upstream on the guest, with a throwaway test ledger, ledger id and checkpoint at their own paths, never the research ledger's path, and no research key (owner, 2026-11-24). `researchroute.Open` has no endpoint setting today (the override in `open` is test-only, `route.go:143-145`), so the probe mode needs its own, applied as `load` applies the override (`route.go:260-261`). `open` always loads a key (`route.go:149-151`), so the probe mode needs its own path that skips key loading. That setting is accepted only together with the test-ledger path and no key file, and it is refused whenever a key file or the research ledger path is configured, so it can never point the research key at another host. Before and after the probe, the research ledger's head and record count are checked unchanged, so the fresh genesis of D59 item 4 holds no reservation before the research pilot. The test ledger is deleted after qualification, and the F33 evidence records the deletion. The real-exchange evidence for V is the research pilot's first V unit, run alone and inspected (D61 item 6).

## Not changed

Screening stays B only. No V on adversarial cases. The product's own V provider choice is a product question. A thesis plan runs V only on the research route.

## Risk

The forwarder is the only new key-bearing code in F33, and it lives in the research service that H1 uses. It needs its own independent review and the full race gate before F33 is qualified.
