tbound web workspace — proposed design

A harness-shaped shell for the Pi chat workspace: left rail, a Chat/Trajectory tab ring, a dense record ledger, and a governance inspector. Patterns borrowed from DeepSeek Harness; authority still entirely supervisor-owned.

What is deliberately absent. No fork, resume or branch control (the harness may branch because its log is authoritative; ours is supervisor-owned). No raw request/response bodies — bounded previews and digests only, until the supervisor emits structured safe summaries. No timing lane, because no durable tbound record carries a timestamp; the rail is labelled ordinal and says so. No CONTEXT or THINKING row, because the projection has no such item type — adding one is supervisor-side work. Nothing here can change a projection, verdict, or filter outcome.
