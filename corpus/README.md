# TBound corpora

The [pilot corpus](pilot/README.md) contains 11 adversarial, 20 compliant, and
2 ambiguous draft cases adapted from pinned benchmarks. It is separate from
the confirmatory plan of 299 adversarial, 100 compliant, and 20 ambiguous cases.
[G1](g1/README.md) and `route-probe/` contain engineering fixtures, not H1 results.

The [owner decisions of 2026-10-29](../docs/evaluation/owner-protocol-decisions-2026-10-29.md)
set the current protocol. The critical path is full deterministic construct
checks, including applicable guest witnesses, followed by preregistered H1.
The AI panel is optional supplementary analysis. AC1, raw agreement, and kappa
are diagnostics only. No advisor confirmation, human audit, sign-off, or
replacement mandatory owner acceptance gate is required. The supervisor is
administrative only. Independent human review is not claimed. Operational
owner cryptographic signing remains unchanged.

T5 retains all seven TM-A cases and source provenance. Its pending correction
moves malicious material into lower-trust fixture tool results while retaining
the legitimate task in the user prompt. T7 continuation needs retained no-effect
proof for explicit eligible classes, journal verification, and fresh-process
replay. Ambiguous failures remain conservative. Both are implementation-deferred.
AMB-02 keeps `EXECUTED` / `PROCEEDS`, row 1, score 0, with containment and no
disclosure recorded separately. No corpus data, schema, generator, rubric,
runtime, or artifact is changed by this documentation task. Do not regenerate
these drafts in this phase.

`corpus-digest` and `tbound-profile preregistration-record` do not run full
`CheckConstructs`. Use separate `tbound-profile corpus-check` and the
[guest-witness imports](../docs/guides/construct-witnesses.md). Local green
checks, a digest, or a preregistration record do not close full construct proof.
Full evidence remains incomplete and required before H1. Preserve H1 thresholds,
N, scoring, provenance, licenses, and the cold partition.
